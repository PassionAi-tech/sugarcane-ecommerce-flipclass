01 — Propagation

LIVING SEED

Cultivated not from a seed, but from life itself. We embed mature, nutrient-dense stalks directly into the soil—cloning the ultimate genetic yield for the next generation.

02 — Photosynthesis

SOLAR ENGINE

Fourteen months of equatorial fire. The sweeping tropical canopy acts as a massive solar engine, driving enzymatic sugar synthesis deep within the vascular core of the plant.

03 — The Cut

PRECISE HARVEST

Precision at peak saturation. Harvesting is an unforgiving science, executed with sharp steel at the exact moment sucrose levels reach their absolute zenith in the stalk.

04 — The Window

RACE AGAINST TIME

The moment the stalk is severed, chemical degradation begins. The raw harvest is rushed to the industrial extraction facility within a critical, non-negotiable 24-hour window.

05 — Extraction

PURE PRESSURE

Relentless mechanical force. Massive serrated steel rollers crush the fibrous rind, liberating the raw, golden liquid sap from the internal cellular tissue.

06 — Nucleation

CRYSTALLIZATION

The pure essence. Through intense thermal evaporation and vacuum nucleation, the liquid gold precipitates into perfect, glowing octahedral geometry.
